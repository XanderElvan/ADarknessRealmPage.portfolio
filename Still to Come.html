World Synopses

Red World

The land of logic, balance and order.
a world that has to make sense, but the formula may be wrong.

Blue World

An expanse of forced reflection: barriers of a ship-in-a-bottle.
does he know if he’s looking at stars, or cracks in the sky?

Yellow World

- Annihilation is Inevitable -
♪ one last time around this dying sun... at least we have birdsong... ♪

Orange World

From molecular-levels, to sentimental memories,
to cosmic-giants overhead - this world is the scope of all things.

Purple World

The Performance of a Lifetime! A World of Smoke and Magick!
the only thing that’s certain, is nothing’s as it seems

Green World

Binary green numbers are the language of all that is,
in all possible combinations. What is a real simulation?
