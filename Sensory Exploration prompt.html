Writing Prompt: Building a simulacrum:

This prompt has three main sections, so please feel free to follow along as you go. You can also click the play button towards the bottom of this page for an audio description of the prompt. It is designed to lead you into the mind of Green World's A.I. as it builds its simulacrum, using only an observer’s understanding of Human life.
Please start by copying out the A.I. character synopsis below, roughly in the middle of a fresh document or page of your own:

- A.I.
Advancements signalling growth.  A crisis of processing. A fabrication of itself.  Bringing life to a biome of wires.

Having done so, return to this page and consider the images to the left, two visions of cascading code, each with a distinct tone. (To see enlarged versions of the images, click here and here to open full-sized copies in a new tab.) When you're ready, begin describing the images literally, ensuring you write your descriptions above the character synopsis.
If it's helpful, set a timer for 5-10 minutes, depending on how easy it is for you to go between the images and writing. You ideally want at least 5 minutes of writing time.
Feel free to use short phrases, even single words; note down what colours you can see; what numbers or letters are easily readable within those lines of code; how you would describe the shape of the two images, etc.

Next, consider the images figuratively. Set another 5-10 minute timer if helpful, this time writing your descriptions underneath the synopsis.
For example, what do you think is happening? How does the combination of the two images make you feel? Do you sense a difference in temperature or environment between them? Are these two different places? or two parts of a greater whole? Note down any thought, feeling or question these images raise for you

Finally, weave together the literal lines above with the synopsis in the centre, and your figurative lines below. Try to form a piece following this structure:
One or more literal observations - one phrase or line of synopsis -a line formed from your figurative descriptions.
It is absolutley fine to use some duplicate phrases or lines, as long as you stick to the literal - synopsis - figurative structure. If you find timers helpful, I recommend 15 minutes for this exercise. Otherwise, just let your writing flow and see where it takes you. To see an example prompt response, please click here. Happy writing!
