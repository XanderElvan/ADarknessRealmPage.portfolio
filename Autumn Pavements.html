Autumn Pavements

Here we are, your hand in mine;
just like the leaves, I’m falling.

Do you see my eyes?
The way they're open wide; never felt like this before.

Here we are, my front door -
have I ever been here before?

Oh, is this the start of love?
Or am I just getting lost?

Here we are, at my front door -
uh-oh, what are the words for this?

Memory Lane sees the night draw in;
no advice, except keep on talking.

These leaves and I, we’re both falling now -
I blush as I invite you inside.

Is this the right move, I’m not sure?
This afternoon was a foreign world -
never felt like this, oh, like this before;
just like the leaves, I’m falling.

Here we are, looking at photographs,
don’t understand why my hands are shaking.

Memory Lane sits on my windowsill -
I laugh out loud; my head’s upside down.

Is this the start of something true,
or am I living a dream out with you?
I’m almost scared to find out,
but I’ll do my best for you -
oh, I’ll do my best for you.

I trust your shadow, I know your hand;
we’re home in this time.
