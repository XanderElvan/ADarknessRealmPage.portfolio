Green World Writing Prompts: Instinct and Constraint

This page contains two writing prompts, inspired by the code of The Green World. The first is a guided freewrite, encouraging an instinctive response to stimuli. The second is a structured exercise, using tighter constraints to place you into the mind of Green World's A.I. as it tries to replicate Human creativity.

For Prompt One, 'What Am I Processing' Freewrite, please click here

Consider the images below. Sit with them for a minute or so, long enough to focus your mind; try to notice any details that catch your eye, or any emotions the images evoke.
When you are ready, open a new page or document and begin a stream of consciousness freewrite with the question, “What am I processing?”

Set a five minute timer, if helpful, or let your creativity arrive at a natural end-point if you prefer.

For Prompt Two, Building a Simulacrum, please click here

Start by copying out the A.I. character synopsis below, roughly in the centre of a new document:

- A.I.
Advancements signalling growth.  A crisis of processing. A fabrication of itself.  Bringing life to a biome of wires.

Next, consider the two images below and describe what you see, first literally, then figuratively. Write your literal observations above the character synopsis, and your figurative descriptions below it. To see a visual representation of this, with some examples, please click here



For literal observations, use short phrases, even single words; note down what colours you can see; what numbers or letters are easily readable within the code, etc.

For Figurative descriptions, note down any feeling or question these images raise for you. What do you think is happening? What emotions do you feel as you view them, etc.
