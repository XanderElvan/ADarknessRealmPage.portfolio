Green World Writing Prompts: Instinct and Constraint

This page contains two writing prompts, inspired by the code of The Green World. The first is a guided freewrite, encouraging an instinctive response to stimuli. The second is a structured exercise, using tighter constraints to invite you into the mind of Green World's A.I,.

To see fullscreen versions of the prompt images, click on each to open them in a new tab.

For Prompt One, 'What Am I Processing' Freewrite,

This prompt aims to precipitate a creative flow-state. The spontaneous creativity that seems to come from the soul.

Consider the images below. Sit with them for a minute or so, long enough to still your mind; try to notice any details that catch your eye, or any emotions the images evoke.
When you are ready, open a new page or document and begin a stream of consciousness freewrite with the question, “What am I processing?”

In a freewrite, you should try to follow your instincts, writing continuously, without judgment; don’t pause to edit or refine as you go. Treat this exercise as a means to enter a creative mind state, which can be returned to later.
Set a five minute timer, if helpful, or let your creativity arrive at a natural end-point if you prefer.

For Prompt Two, Building a Simulacrum,

With this prompt, we invite you to reshape previously written words into new poetry. Incapsulating the tone of Green World's A.I,, as it tries to replicate human creativity.

First, consider the two images below and describe what you can literally see. You can use short phrases, even single words. For example:

  • - Note down each different colour you can see
  • - Note down any particular numbers, letters or symbols within the lines of code that strike you.
  • - Note down any shapes or patterns you notice.

Next, copy out the A.I, character synopsis below, underneath your literal observations:

- A.I,
Advancements signalling growth.  A crisis of processing.
A fabrication of itself. 
Bringing life to a biome of wires.

Having done so, return to the two images of code. This time, to describe them figuratively. Using phrases, or full sentances. For example:

  • - Note down any feeling or question these images raise for you
  • - What do you think the intent behind these two instances of code is?
  • - Do any metaphors or similes strike you as you survey each image?

Finally, try to construct a poem by moving top to bottom. Build lines using one literal observation, one phrase from the synopsis, and one figurative description. For example:

Letters amongst the binary, bringing life
to the code of functions and processes.

I see a question mark. A crisis of processing.
Code is cascading; fresh inputs needed.

It’s fine to use the same words or phrases multiple times, and to adapt your original descriptions.
If you would prefer to combine your descriptions without incorporating the synopsis, please do so.
