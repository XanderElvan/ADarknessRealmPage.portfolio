Kind of Love

From Anastasia Album

Woven Realms Ltd. 2025

Kind of Love

A worn-down doorbell welcome
hums a September entrance.
The porch-light not aforementioned
is following the knocks of a place I forgot, but
that’s just one kind of love -
time will find another one.

Photos frame recollections;
sofa-gone indent-pressings -
both blamed my slept-in foundations
with a bed that I can shift
and you stepped in to make them.
Well, we made some kind of love -
I saw space form above.

I am what’s left in the opening moments;
I’m every line that somehow slips away;
I’m every panel that patterns out for your steps;
I’m all that remains, and a day that’s gone to waste.

A sunrise bus stop shelter;
our scheduled park-bench adventures.
My slow-adjusting sight receptors
are following the mornings that come after cushioned fallings;
I fell in some kind of love -
with bruises warm to the touch.

A glass of uncharted waters;
I’m filled with filtered-out thoughts, and
an ice-cube-tenth of contentment
is all that I pretend
and I never once intended
to give a cold kind of love;
my palms are desperately cupped.

Well, I’m no stranger to the strongest cost of kindness;
my sudden blueprints were encountered in a single act;
my distant viewpoint, out-focused and unaccounted for;
looking back, I’ll pay it forward a little more.
