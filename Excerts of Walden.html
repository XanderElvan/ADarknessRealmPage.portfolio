Excerpts of Walden

Here; here is life,
an experiment, to a great extent, untried by me.

I shouldn’t talk so much,
oh about myself, if there were anyone else I knew as well.

You can’t kill time without hurting eternity, no how,
oh, and I know how.
I can’t improve the nick of time,
notch it on my stick and stand out of line -
this time: it’s mine.

Pain, well I promise to pay,
a quick visit tomorrow, before I die today.

So I’ll survey this world through scopes and shapes -
never natural eyes; we’re all hope-lost satellites.

Men say that they know many things,
but lo, they have only taken wings,
if they should feel the springs,
surely they’d rise high -
for an ethereal life.

Love, and arts, and sciences,
and a thousand or so appliances -
oh, the wind that blows
is all anybody knows;
even Henry Thoreau.

Here is life,
an experiment, untried by me.
