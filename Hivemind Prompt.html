Writing from the point of view of a hive-mind:

In this prompt, we invite you to consider a new perspective: How does it feel to be part of a cosmic hive-mind, speaking with one voice while traversing the vacuum of space?

This exercise will focus on using the first-person plural pronoun, we, in a stream of consciousness.
Please press the play button on the soundscape to your left, and observe the accompanying images. (If you would like to see enlarged versions, which will open in a new tab, please click here, and click here).

Start writing as soon as you feel able (using the document below if you wish), responding to the stimuli as one, collective, 'We.'
Use whichever form feels natural to you, the only rule is, don't edit yourself as you go!
(The soundscape is set to loop by default, so you can leave it playing throughout, or pause at any time.)

If a starting point would be helpful, try beginning your stream of consciousness, 'We are The Particulates,' or by using some of these phrases: We see- We feel- We hear- We judge- We must- We make-

Alternatively, consider the following synopsis, and think about what their motivations might be:

- The Particulates -
/ collective judgement / of oneself / observers of all / orbital routines /
restraint from intervention
