Welcome to The Darkness Realm Example Pages:

Here, poetry, songs and soundscapes intertwine to create combined arts pieces and prompts.
Below are three worlds, each with their own themes, characters and sound.
Where will you choose to start?

The Orange World

From molecular-levels,
to sentimental memories,
to cosmic-giants overhead - this world is the scope
of all things.

The Green World

Binary green numbers are
the language of all that is,
in all possible combinations.
What is a real simulation?

The Red World

The land of logic, balance and order.
A world that has to make sense, but
the formula may be wrong.
