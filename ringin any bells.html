Is it ringing any bells?

From A Matter of Time EP

Woven Realms Ltd. 2025

Is it ringing any bells?

Is it ringing any bells? Like you’ve been here before?
I couldn’t even tell, I’ve never felt so sure.

Learning as I go, without you by my side -
don’t amount to much, there’s nothing much to life.

Skip further on ahead, but don’t jump to conclusions -
the odds are stacked against me, but I’ve done the calculations.

Time Machines - think of them time and time again -
Time Machines - flashing before my very eyes;
a chance to bring you back to me.

I’ll build this prototype, it works on paper and in theory -
with an hourglass in hand, I can see it all so clearly.

A single grain of rice, squared by sixty-four -
I heard the faintest sound; ripples reach the shore.

Time Machines - don’t get washed away in the rain -
Time Machines - forge a way across these waves,
if the universe abides.

I just saw my life - inside a drop of rain -
and all my past selves missed your presence here today.

“Wouldn't change a thing, I couldn’t if I tried.”
That’s what you said to me, moments before you died.

There’s whistles and there’s chimes, there’s echoes of your ghost -
beyond the point of no return, all I’m left with is:

Time Machines - the numbers aren’t adding up it seems -
but Time Machines are a way to crack the code at last.
Correct me if I’m wrong...
