Prompt: Get to know your setting:


Setting can often be a character in itself. Some are cruel, unforgiving places to escape or overcome. Others are like old friends, welcoming you back.

Below there are two lists of questions, split across two perspectives. The first set are written for you, the writer, to consider. The second are adressed directly to your setting, regarding it as a character in its own right.

You can switch between the two sets of questions by clicking the 'Change Perspective' button at the bottom of the list. If a question doesn't seem relevant to your setting, feel free to skip it, or adapt it.

Questions for you, the writer, to consider:

How would you describe the atmosphere, using ten words or less?

Is this place unique? Or one of many such places?

What can be smelt in the air; what sounds can be heard on the wind?

Is today a day like any other?

Is this place natural, or has it been imposed upon the landscape?

What stories are told about this place, and are any of them true?

Has this place ever experienced a tragedy?
